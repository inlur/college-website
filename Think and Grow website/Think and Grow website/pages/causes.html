Causes of climate change

Human activities release greenhouse gases that trap heat in the atmosphere.

Burning fuels

Coal, oil and gas used for electricity, heating and transport release carbon dioxide.

Deforestation

Cutting down forests releases stored carbon and removes trees that absorb carbon dioxide.

Consumption

Making, transporting and throwing away products uses energy and creates emissions.
